Star Merge (별 머지) Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026 · 한국어

Nine to Nap Studio ("we") respects the privacy of everyone who plays the mobile game Star Merge (별 머지, the "App"). This policy explains what information the App handles and how. If the Korean version and this translation differ, the Korean version prevails.

1. Summary

  • The App has no sign-up or login and never asks for your name, phone number or email.
  • Your game progress is stored only on your device. We do not operate any server.
  • The App shows ads through Google AdMob, which processes some information such as the advertising ID.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

2. Information handled and why

(1) Stored only on your device — not collected by us

  • What: game progress such as the board, collection, energy, number of Rainbow Stars, selected theme and today's ad count
  • Why: so you can continue your game
  • This information never leaves your device.

(2) Processed automatically by the ad SDK — Google

  • What: advertising ID (Android ad ID), app set ID, IP address (which may be used to estimate approximate location), device and diagnostic information (such as app launch time and errors), and ad interactions (ad impressions, taps, video views)
  • Why: to serve ads, measure ad performance (analytics) and prevent fraud
  • The Google Mobile Ads SDK processes this when the App loads ads. We do not receive it in a form that identifies you.

3. Retention

  • On-device data: until you uninstall the App or clear its data
  • Data processed by the ad SDK: as described in Google's Privacy Policy

4. Deletion

We keep no personal information. On-device game data is deleted immediately when you uninstall the App or clear its data in Android Settings → Apps → 별 머지 → Storage → Clear data.

5. Third parties

Recipient Google LLC (Google AdMob)
Purpose Serving ads, measuring ad performance, preventing fraud
Data Advertising ID, app set ID, IP address, device and diagnostic information, ad interactions
Retention According to Google's Privacy Policy
International transfer May be transferred to countries where Google operates data centers, including the United States, when the App loads ads, over an encrypted (TLS) connection

See the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

6. Personalized ads and consent

  • Users in regions that require consent, such as the EEA and the UK, are asked for consent through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP).
  • To opt out of personalized ads, reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads.

7. Your rights

You may ask to access, correct, delete or stop the processing of your personal information at any time. Because we keep no user data, you can manage on-device data as described in section 4, and ad-related data through the advertising ID settings in section 6 and your Google Account settings. For anything else, email us below.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not collect information that identifies a person, such as a name or contact details, from anyone.

9. Security

We do not operate servers that store user information. Data processed by the ad SDK is encrypted in transit with TLS.
